FAQ

Questions we get asked most.

Something else? Ask us directly.

What does NSZ Group do?

NSZ Group is a Cape Town web and IT studio. We design and build websites and web apps, build online stores with PayFast or Stripe checkout, design logos, posters and social media content, and set up hosting, domains, business email, office networks and CCTV for South African businesses.

How much does a website cost?

A single-page website is R1 500, a business website of up to five pages is R4 500, and a premium website with unlimited pages is R8 500, all once-off. Online stores start at R6 500 plus R250 a month for hosting.

How long does a website take?

Most small sites take days rather than months. We confirm a fixed timeline in writing with your quote, based on the number of pages and features.

Do you only work with clients in Cape Town?

No. We are based in Cape Town and work with businesses across South Africa remotely. Networking and CCTV installations are done on site.

How do I get a quote?

Fill in the form on our contact page, WhatsApp us on 063 437 1374, or email nszgroupbussines@gmail.com. We usually reply within one business day.

Send a few lines about your project. We reply with a plan and a fixed quote, usually within one business day.
