FAQ

Branding & social media: common questions.

How much does a logo cost?

The Logo Package is R2 500 and includes three concepts, three revisions per concept, vector files in all formats and brand guidelines. A full Brand Identity is R7 500 and a Brand Refresh is R12 500.

How much does a poster cost and how fast is it?

Posters start at R150 with a 3–5 day turnaround. The Startup package is R350 with print-ready files in 2–3 days, and the Business package is R800 for up to three designs in 1–2 days.

How much is social media management?

Social Starter is R2 500 a month for 8 posts on 2 platforms. Social Pro is R5 500 a month for 16 posts on 4 platforms with ad management. Social Enterprise is R12 500 a month for 24+ posts.
