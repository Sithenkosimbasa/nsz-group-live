FAQ

Websites & web apps: common questions.

How much does a website cost?

A single-page website is R1 500 once-off. A business website of up to five pages is R4 500, and a premium website with unlimited pages and CMS integration is R8 500. Web apps with accounts, bookings or dashboards are quoted per project.

Do I own my website’s code?

Yes. Every site is custom-built and the files are handed over to you, so you are never locked into a page builder or a platform subscription to keep your site online.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every site is designed mobile-first and tested on phone, tablet and desktop before launch.
