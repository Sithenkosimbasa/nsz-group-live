Privacy policy

How NSZ Group (Pty) Ltd collects, uses and protects personal information, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Last updated 10 October 2026.

1. Who we are

NSZ Group (Pty) Ltd (“NSZ Group”, “we”, “us”) is a web and IT services company based in Cape Town, South Africa. We are the responsible party for personal information collected through nszgroup.co.za.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, phone number, business name and project details when you use our contact form, email us or message us on WhatsApp.
  • Project and billing information: requirements, timelines, budgets and invoicing details when you become a client. Card payments are handled by third-party payment providers; we never store full card details.
  • Technical information: our hosting provider records standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and reliability.

3. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google’s servers and shares your IP address with Google when you visit.

4. How we use your information

  • To reply to enquiries and prepare quotes.
  • To deliver, host and support the services you hire us for.
  • To send invoices and keep records required by South African law.

We process personal information on the basis of your consent, to perform a contract with you, to comply with legal obligations, or for our legitimate business interests, as allowed by POPIA.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run our business, such as our website host (Netlify), email provider, payment processors and, where a project requires it, the database provider used to build your system (Supabase). We also share information when the law requires it. Some of these providers store data outside South Africa; we only use providers with adequate data-protection safeguards.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information only as long as needed to respond and follow up. Client and financial records are kept for as long as tax and company law require.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls and reputable hosting providers. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. If a data breach affects your information, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

8. Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, correct or delete it, object to its processing, or withdraw consent. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.

9. PAIA manual and Information Officer

Our Promotion of Access to Information Act (PAIA) manual is available on request. To make a request or to contact our Information Officer, email nszgroupbussines@gmail.com or call 063 437 1374.

10. Links to other websites

Our site links to projects we have built and to third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with its updated date.
